# PRODUCT.md — Base360 Landing Page

## Register
brand

## What it is
Base360 is one system that unifies all of a B2C brand's communication (every social comment, DM, WhatsApp, SMS, call, email), automates the work with AI agents, captures and nurtures subscribers, and turns every conversation into a tracked lead. One place to reach customers, run the team, and close.

## Users
Founders and marketing leads of B2C brands (fashion, beauty, food, DTC e-commerce). They are drowning in Instagram/TikTok comments and DMs across five apps. Not deeply technical. They buy outcomes, not features: answered customers, captured leads, closed sales.

## The story the page must tell (from the client brief)
One comment, handled end to end by AI:
1. A customer comments on a TikTok post: "how much is this?"
2. The AI replies to the comment publicly, then slides into their DMs
3. In the DM, the AI explains the offering and answers questions
4. The contact is added to the CRM as a high-intent lead
5. The AI places a call with an AI voice to walk them through the product
6. They're enrolled into marketing emails and nurtured until they buy

The feeling: a single comment that would normally be missed becomes a closed customer, automatically, across every channel, with no one lifting a finger.

## Creative concept: "The Night Shift"
The page is the story of one night. At 23:47 a comment lands, the founder is asleep, and by morning Base360 has turned it into a sale. Scrolling moves time forward: deep night ink gives way to dawn and then warm morning light. Base360 is the light that stays on.

## Brand voice words
Nocturnal, effortless, wide-awake.

## Tone
Confident, warm, a little cinematic. Speaks in outcomes and moments, never in feature jargon. No hype words ("revolutionary", "supercharge").

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS purple-on-white with icon card grids
- Neon-on-black "AI startup" dark mode
- Editorial serif magazine affectation
- Hero-metric dashboards with floating gradient blobs

## Strategic principles
- The six-step hero example is the spine of the page, not a section among others
- Every feature is shown as a realistic product moment (a comment, a DM, a lead card, a call), not described
- Color carries the narrative arc: night, dawn, morning
- Conversion: two CTAs only ("Book a demo" primary), repeated at hero and final morning section
