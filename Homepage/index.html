How I approach

Thoughts behind the process.

01

Understanding before solving

I like understanding the real problem before exploring solutions — through user behaviour, product context, research and conversations with the right people.

02

Thinking beyond individual screens

I care about how experiences connect across the full journey — how flows, interactions and small details come together to create a cohesive experience.

03

Iteration shapes better products

I see design as an evolving process shaped by feedback, testing and continuous refinement rather than a single final answer.

04

Thoughtful over trendy

I don't believe every trend works for every product. I prefer approaches that make experiences feel clearer, more usable and intentional in their own context.

About me

Hey!

I am a product designer based in Berlin with five years in e-commerce and travel. Most recently at DERTOUR Group, working across Inntravel, Inghams, and Explore on B2C flows. Before that at Loopingo, where I designed both the consumer product and the B2B tools our clients used.

My background is industrial engineering and marketing — not a typical path into product design, but it taught me to think across teams and understand how business goals, user needs, and operational reality have to meet in the same flow. When I'm not working: traveling, computer games, and illustrations — a few are hiding in the footer.
